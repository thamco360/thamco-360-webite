The Spatial Pipeline

Five Steps to a Living 3D Space

From high-resolution field capture to full 3D reconstruction and interactive web delivery.

01 · FIELD CAPTURE

8K HDR Spatial Scan

Our scan technicians deploy high-resolution 360° HDR camera rigs and LiDAR sensors. Every corner, architectural beam, and natural lighting condition is digitized with sub-millimeter precision.

02 · 3D PROCESSING

Immersive 3D Reconstruction

We process raw imagery into a realistic, navigable 3D volume. Surfaces lose their flat 2D nature and become spatial spaces with true optical depth.

03 · 3D MODELING

3D Architectural Geometry

Detailed 3D models and realistic textures are generated automatically, ready for use in AR, VR, and web applications.

04 · HOTSPOT INTEGRATION

Spatial Hotspots & Analytics

Embed interactive product tags, floorplan radar nodes, audio guides, and e-commerce checkout links directly inside the 3D space.

05 · WEBGL DELIVERY

Scroll-Driven Web Launch

Your tour launches directly in any modern browser at 60fps — desktop, smartphone, or VR headset. Single-line embed code for instant site integration.
