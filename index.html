Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Thamco360 based?

Thamco360 is based in Bengaluru, India, and travels to the client's property for every 360° capture — there's no showroom to visit, the scan crew comes to you.

What is a 360° virtual tour?

A 360° virtual tour is a scroll- or drag-driven walkthrough built from high-resolution panoramic photography, letting a website visitor look around and move between rooms in a browser exactly as they would in person.

How long does a Thamco360 shoot take?

Thamco360 deploys a scan crew within 48 hours of booking; most residential and commercial properties are fully captured in a single same-day visit.

Can Thamco360 build a website around the virtual tour?

Yes — Thamco360 also offers website development and immersive web 3D development, so the finished tour can be embedded directly into a custom site rather than only hosted as a standalone link.
