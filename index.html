Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Thamco360 based?

Thamco360 is based in Bengaluru, India, and travels to the client's property for every 360° capture — there's no showroom to visit, the scan crew comes to you.

What is a 360° virtual tour?

A 360° virtual tour is a scroll- or drag-driven walkthrough built from high-resolution panoramic photography, letting a website visitor look around and move between rooms in a browser exactly as they would in person.

How long does a Thamco360 shoot take?

Thamco360 deploys a scan crew within 48 hours of booking; most residential and commercial properties are fully captured in a single same-day visit.

Can Thamco360 build a website around the virtual tour?

Yes — Thamco360 also offers website development and immersive web 3D development, so the finished tour can be embedded directly into a custom site rather than only hosted as a standalone link.

What industries does Thamco360 work with?

Thamco360 captures real estate and residential property, hospitality including hotels and resorts, weddings and event venues, and museums and galleries. Published client work also covers hospitals, dental clinics, cafés and live-music venues in Bengaluru.

What is the difference between a 360° photo and a stitched virtual tour?

A raw 360° capture comes off the camera as two circular fisheye frames that are not yet joined. Stitching aligns and blends those frames into a single seamless equirectangular sphere, which is what a visitor can then look around inside. You can compare the two directly in the raw-versus-stitched slider on this page.

Are Thamco360 tours published on Google?

Yes. Thamco360 is a Google Trusted photography provider and publishes finished 360° tours directly to Google Business Profile, so the tour appears in Google Search and Google Maps alongside the business listing. The portfolio on this page embeds live Google-hosted tours.
